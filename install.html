🦖 Install TReps on your phone

TReps runs as a home-screen app - no app store needed. Installing takes about 30 seconds, and it is required for notifications (daily reminders, squad pushes) to work on iPhone.

Open TReps

Note: if you opened the TReps link inside WhatsApp's built-in browser, it cannot be added to your Home Screen from there - open this page in Safari (iPhone) or Chrome (Android) first.

iPhone (Safari)

  1. Open the TReps link above in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow pointing up - in the toolbar at the bottom of the screen, or in the ⋯ menu).
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add in the top-right corner.
  5. Open TReps from the new icon on your home screen (not from Safari).
  6. In onboarding, tap Enable notifications and then Allow - done. Reminders and squad pushes will now reach you even when the app is closed.

Android (Chrome)

  1. Open the TReps link above in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu in the top-right corner.
  3. Tap Add to Home screen (or Install app), then confirm.
  4. Open TReps from the new home-screen icon and allow notifications when asked.

Why install?

On iPhone, websites in a browser tab cannot send notifications at all - Apple only allows push for home-screen apps. Installed, TReps also opens full-screen like a regular app and keeps your streak, tests and history on the device.

Already have the icon? You do not need to reinstall for updates - the app refreshes itself. If notifications ever stop, open Settings inside TReps and check that Push notifications is on.

Note: removing the app from your Home Screen deletes its data on this device. You can save a backup file any time from Settings, in the Your data - backup & restore section.
