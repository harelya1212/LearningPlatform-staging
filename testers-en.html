📱 Screens & Their Purpose

📊Home

Your hub. Weekly progress, points, day streaks, the Daily Challenge card, quick access to tests, and your squads. Everything in one place.

⚡Challenge

Daily Challenge: 10 questions, 3 minutes on the clock, once a day. Quick Test: 10 questions, no pressure. Weak Spots: the questions you most need to strengthen - the system identifies them automatically.

📚Tests

All 152 tests. You can set a target date for each test (tap the date), and the app builds a personalized study plan. Cards with red dots = new tests not yet opened.

🛡️Squads

A feature you reach from inside the app - Squads is not a bottom-bar tab. The social heart: create a squad, invite friends with a code, compete in Battles (10 head-to-head questions), send cheers 👏, and see who's studying right now in real time.

🔔Reminders

Smart daily review reminders, notifications for battles and cheers from friends. Quiet hours configurable.

⚙️Settings

Profile (name, avatar), text size, dyslexia font, voice narration, sounds, theme. Everything saved locally on device.

🧠 How It Works Behind the Scenes

  • Spaced Repetition: every answer updates when the question returns. Got it wrong? It comes back soon. Got it right 3 times? It moves further away.
  • Chunks of 10: never more than 10 questions in a row without a break. You can stop anytime.
  • Flood protection: even after a week away, you won't get 500 questions at once - max 100 per session.
  • Ads: only between chunks, never mid-question. Max one per session.
